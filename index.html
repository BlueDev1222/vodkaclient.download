What it is

Built to be useful.

Building and utility are core parts of Vodka Client, especially for large projects and anarchy gameplay.

Our approach

FAQ

Quick answers.

What version is supported?

Vodka Client V1.0.0 currently supports Minecraft 1.21.11 on Fabric.

Will older 1.21 versions be supported?

1.21.4 through 1.21.10 are planned next, followed by 26.1 and 26.2.

Will NeoForge be supported?

NeoForge is planned for the future after the Fabric versions are stable.

Does Vodka Client use AI?

Yes. Vodka Client uses AI-assisted development alongside human-written code, manual debugging, and fixes by the developers. Read the full explanation on the Why Vodka page.
