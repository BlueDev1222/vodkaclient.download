Built for Anarchy. Made for Builders.

Vodka Client

One client.
Multiple playstyles.

Vodka is being designed around the ways people actually play anarchy Minecraft, with a major focus on building while still providing utilities for exploration and combat.

⛏

Building

Building is the core focus of Vodka Client. We're working on tools designed to make massive bases, highways, monuments, infrastructure and other large-scale anarchy projects easier to create and manage.

◈

Anarchy

Utilities designed around huge worlds, long-distance travel, dangerous environments and the unpredictable nature of anarchy servers.

⚔

PvP

Vodka will include configurable combat utilities for players who want to fight while exploring or protecting their projects.

✦

Crystal PvP

Crystal PvP utilities are planned for players who want a client capable of handling high-intensity anarchy combat.

◉

Render

Visual utilities to make navigation, building, exploration and understanding your surroundings easier.

⚙

Customization

Configure Vodka around your playstyle instead of forcing yourself into one preset way of playing.

Builder First

Built for people who actually build.

Most anarchy clients heavily prioritize combat. Vodka is being built differently. Building and large-scale projects are one of our primary focuses.

Build bigger.

Whether you're repairing infrastructure, constructing highways, creating giant bases, working with schematics or building at spawn, Vodka aims to provide tools that make repetitive and difficult building work significantly easier.
