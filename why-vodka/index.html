Why Vodka Client?

We are not going to pretend AI is not part of the project. It is. But AI is a tool, not a replacement for knowing how to code.

AI-assisted. Human fixed. Free.

Yes, we use AI.

We acknowledge that AI is used to help make Vodka Client. We use it to speed up development, generate ideas, assist with repetitive work, and help build features faster.

No, AI does not do everything.

AI can break things, misunderstand Minecraft internals, create bad logic, and fail on edge cases. That is where actual coding knowledge matters. We manually fix bugs, rewrite code, debug problems, and build things AI cannot reliably produce on its own.

Both developers know how to code.

DEVXON and infernoxlt are not just pressing generate and shipping whatever comes out. Human knowledge is used to review, fix, change, and maintain the client.

And Vodka Client is free.

Our position is simple: if a client can offer the same kind of utility, building, anarchy, movement, and PvP features without charging you for access, why pay just because someone put a price tag on theirs?
